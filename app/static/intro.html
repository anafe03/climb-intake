The problem

Support requests arrive as free text, from email, forms and chat, and nobody labels them. Someone has to read each one and decide what it is, how fast it needs an answer, whether someone senior should know, and which team gets it. That costs time and money. Faster, more accurate triage gets each request to the right team sooner, and that leads to better outcomes.

What this does

It decides five things about each request and explains each one. Click any to see how.

Every decision is logged with its reason.
