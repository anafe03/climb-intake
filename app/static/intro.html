The problem

Support requests arrive as free text, from email, forms and chat, and nobody labels them. Someone has to read each one and decide what it is, how fast it needs an answer, whether someone senior should know, and which team gets it. That costs time and money. Faster, more accurate triage gets each request to the right team sooner, and that leads to better outcomes.

What this does

It reads each request and decides four things, and it explains every one:

Who sent itthe company if it is named, or a clearly marked best guess
What kind of requestone of eight categories
How urgentone of four levels
Does someone need to knowfive topics always go to a person

Then it sends the request to the right team, and records every decision and the reason for it, so any routing can be checked later.
